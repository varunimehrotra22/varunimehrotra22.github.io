About

Namaste, I'm Varuni

My journey to sound healing has been shaped by many different chapters of my life. I grew up in India, between Delhi, Chennai and Bangalore. I always loved mathematics, which led me to a Bachelor's in Mathematics at St. Stephen's College, then postgraduate studies at Leiden University and the University of Padova.

I settled in London and spent 11 years in financial services. It taught me a great deal, but over time I found myself drawn to a slower, more conscious way of living. I recently left my corporate career to give myself the space to pursue this passion.

Yoga, Ayurveda and sound healing have each shaped how I care for myself, and retreats around the world brought together two things I love: travelling and meeting like-minded people. Sound healing now feels like a natural coming together of it all: my curiosity, my love of learning, and my wish to create more calm and stillness in everyday life.

Through my sessions, I hope to create a peaceful, welcoming space where you can step away from the busyness of daily life and simply have some time for yourself.

The name 'Varuni' carries an ancient Vedic association with water and cosmic harmony, themes that echo the natural rhythm and restorative qualities of sound.

Questions

Frequently asked

About sound healing

What is sound healing, and how does it work?

Sound healing uses the tones and vibrations of instruments like singing bowls, chimes and other resonant instruments to create a calming, immersive experience that can support relaxation and mindful rest.

What's the difference between Standard and Premium?

A Standard session is 60 minutes and includes gentle stretching, guided meditation and sound healing. A Premium session is 90 minutes and adds pranayama and chanting.

What might I notice after a session?

People often describe feeling calmer, lighter and more rested afterwards. Everyone's experience is different.

What should I wear?

Loose, comfortable clothing you can fully relax in, with a layer for warmth.

How often should I book?

It depends on what you're looking for. Some people book weekly or monthly as an ongoing practice; others prefer occasional sessions. If you're not sure, a few sessions is a good way to find your rhythm.

Can I come if I'm pregnant or have a health condition?

If you're pregnant, have epilepsy or a seizure disorder, a pacemaker or other medical device, or another relevant health condition, please tell me when booking. It's a good idea to check with your healthcare professional first.

Does sound healing replace medical treatment?

No. Sound healing is a complementary wellness practice that supports relaxation and wellbeing. It isn't a substitute for medical or psychological care.

Packages & booking

Do unused package sessions expire?

Yes. Sessions must be used within the validity window for your package: 4 months for Monthly and Fortnightly Rhythm, 6 months for Open Rhythm.

What if I need to cancel?

Plans change, and I understand. As each session is reserved especially for you, payments are non-refundable. You're welcome to reschedule with at least 24 hours' notice.

What if I need to reschedule?

The same terms apply each time. For example, if you give less than 24 hours' notice twice during a 3-session package, two sessions would be forfeited.

Can a shared session be split between people?

Shared sessions are booked and paid for by one person for the whole group, and can't be split into separate bookings. Full payment is due at booking, whatever the final number of attendees.

Can I gift a session?

Yes, sessions make a lovely gift. Get in touch and I'll help you arrange the details, including timing and any preferences for the recipient.
